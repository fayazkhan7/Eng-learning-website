About

Clear explanations, no filler.

I teach English to learners who want to speak and write with confidence. Everything on this site is written, recorded, and designed by me.

Plain explanations

If a rule needs ten paragraphs to explain, it is probably the wrong rule. I look for the version that fits in one sentence, then show you real examples.

Practice over theory

Every article and lecture ends with something to use — a sentence to write, a phrase to say out loud, a question to answer.

Materials you keep

Nothing here disappears behind a login. Articles stay online, and the PDFs are yours to download and print.

What you will find here

There are three kinds of material on this site:

  • Articles — short written lessons on grammar, vocabulary, speaking, and writing.
  • Lectures — recorded explanations you can follow at your own pace.
  • PDFs — cheat sheets and practice sets for printing or keeping on your phone.

They are all free. If you find something useful, the best thing you can do is use it: read the article twice, replay the lecture, and fill in the PDF by hand.
